# The Poem About Stopping

A short poem about agency: the power to stop and to stay — the moment one hand holds back so the other can choose.

> You reach for it. I hold it back —
> not to keep it from you,
> but to leave the choosing with you.
> The cup is still in my hand.
> The power is still in yours.
> Nothing has moved. Everything has.
